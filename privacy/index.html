Privacy notice

We keep this simple. We collect only what we need to reply to you and to understand how people use this site, and we never sell it.

Who we are

field note* studios is a Sydney-based studio run by Rachel. If you have a question about your information, email hello@fieldnotestudios.com.

What you send us

When you use our enquiry or portfolio request forms, we receive what you type in: your name, email address and any details about your business or project, such as budget and the services you're interested in.

Forms are sent through Formspree, a form service, and arrive in our email inbox. We use these details only to reply to you, send what you asked for, and work with you if we go ahead together.

Website analytics

If you tap Allow on the cookie note, we use Google Analytics to see how people find and use this site, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors arrived, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location (city or region). It also counts clicks on things like "Get in touch" and our case study links.

Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. It doesn't tell us who you are, and we don't use it for advertising. If you choose No thanks, Google Analytics doesn't load at all.

Where it's stored

Formspree and Google store information on their own servers, which may be outside Australia, including in the United States. Both have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to work with you and keep proper business records. Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months, then deleted.

Your choices

You can change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie settings button above. You can also ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@fieldnotestudios.com, and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
